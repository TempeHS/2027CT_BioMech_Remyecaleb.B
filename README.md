# HeartBEET

# 1  Overview
The device is a durable, wearable heart monitor that alerts the user and family/first responders when the user's heart-rate drops. It will be durable and water-proof to work effectively during sport and outdoor activities.

It's needed for people who want a heart monitor that's effective with the sweat, heat and movement of sport.

The monitor will make Phillip feel comfortable enough to play tennis again.

# 2  Patient Profile
The monitor is for Phillip, a 66 year old male and ex-tennis-coach who hasn't been able to play after fears from his last heart attack. It needs to give him peace of mind and safety to play tennis again.
